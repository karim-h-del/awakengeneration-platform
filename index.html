The Manifesto of the Awakened Generation

Awaken Generation
  • English Curriculum
  • Curriculum Français
  • المنهج بالعربية

In the name of the Creator of Existence, who encoded the cosmos with law, the soul with trust, and the intellect with light:

We were born into dust, but not into blindness.
We inherited ritual, but not revelation.
We were handed slogans, but we seek signs.

We declare that:

1. Revelation Is a Code, Not a Costume

The Qur’an is not a book of choreography. It is a cosmic syllabus.
It speaks of hydrogen, gravity, entropy, and emergence.
It commands us to read — not just recite. To reflect — not just repeat. To decode — not just decorate.

[Read more: Scientific and historical context]

2. Sunna Is Memory, Not Revelation

We honor the Prophet ﷺ as the transmitter of divine light.
But we do not confuse biography with divine speech.
Ibn Ishaq compiled, Al-Shafi‘i codified — but only God legislates.
We reclaim the Qur’an as the sole source of revelation.

[Read more: Historical evolution of Sunna]

3. Mosques Must Awaken or Be Emptied

We reject stagnation.
We do not abandon community — we abandon conformity.
If a mosque teaches ritual without reason, it is a shell. If it silences inquiry, it is a tomb.
We will build sanctuaries of thought, not just walls of stone.

[Read more: Institutional critique and reform]

4. Haman Is the Architect of Oppression

Pharaoh is the tyrant. Qarun is the financier.
But Haman is the most dangerous — the builder of false doctrine.
He hides in ministries, media, and curricula. He designs systems that enslave minds while praising freedom.
We will expose him. We will dismantle his architecture.

[Read more: Qur’anic archetypes and modern systems]

5. Consciousness Is the Journey Beyond Light

We are not flesh — we are data-bearing souls.
At death, our consciousness travels faster than light, through wormholes of divine design.
We will be folded into the scroll of existence, as promised in Surah Al-Anbiya.
Time will collapse. Truth will unfold. Judgment will be just.

[Read more: Physics, resurrection, and eschatology]

6. Forgiveness Is the First Step of Revolution

We do not blame our parents. We do not curse the ignorant.
We forgive — and we rebuild.
We honor the verse: “And We have enjoined upon man [care] for his parents…”
Even when they strive against truth, we respond with kindness — and correction.

[Read more: Ethics of intergenerational awakening]

7. We Are the Generation of Awakening

We will not be pacified by aid, distracted by slogans, or divided by sects.
We are not Sunni, Shia, Salafi, or Sufi — we are seekers.
We are not Arab, Amazigh, African, or European — we are human.
We are not followers of ritual — we are followers of revelation.

[Read more: Global solidarity and epistemic sovereignty]

🕊️ Beyond Labels: The Religion of Submission

True religion is not a label.
It is not “Christian,” “Jewish,” or “Muslim.”
It is the original way of obedience — revealed when the heavens and the earth responded, “We came willingly.”

📖 Qur’anic Foundation

“Indeed, those who believed and those who were Jews and the Christians and the Sabians — those who believed in Allah and the Last Day and did righteousness — will have their reward with their Lord…”
(Surah Al-Baqara 2:62)

The phrase “those who believed” refers to a subset from each group — those who truly submitted. They form the real Muslim community: those who believe in God, the Last Day, and do good.

🧠 Belief as Inquiry

To believe in the unseen is not to accept blindly — it is to seek, decode, and transform. The unseen is Anbaa’ — news — and must be interpreted into objective truth that uplifts humanity.

🌟 Eternal Reward

“Indeed, those for whom the best [reward] has preceded from Us — they will be far removed from it. They will not hear its faintest sound, while they are, in that which their souls desire, abiding eternally.”
(Surah Al-Anbiya 21:101–102)

🧬 Identity Is Obedience

We are not sects. We are not slogans. We are the generation that submits — willingly, consciously, universally.

The Sleep of a Learned Man

“The sleep of a learned man is better than the standing of an ignorant man.”

This is not a metaphor for laziness. It is a rebuke of ritualism without understanding.
Motion without meaning is not worship. Silence filled with insight outweighs noise devoid of reflection.

For 1447 years, the message of Muhammad ﷺ has echoed. Yet many nations — especially Arab ones — remain shackled by inherited forms.
They recite: “Indeed, Allah does not change the condition of a people until they change what is in themselves.” (Qur’an 13:11)
But they have not changed.

Their mosques remain static. Their sermons repeat inherited scripts. Their institutions cling to the four madhhabs as if they were divine.
Echoing the cry of the enemies of Noah: “Never abandon your gods: Wadd, Suwa’, Ya’uq, and Nasr.” (Qur’an 71:23)

Behind every Pharaoh is a Haman — the builder of towers, the enforcer of orthodoxy.
Today’s Haman is not a minister of bricks, but a guardian of inherited jurisprudence.
He silences inquiry, institutionalizes stagnation, and calls it tradition.

We reject inherited ritualism.
We reclaim revelation as a living code — one that evolves, provokes, and awakens.
We honor the sleep of the learned man, because his stillness is filled with light.
We challenge the standing of the ignorant man, because his motion is hollow.

Le Sommeil du Savant

« Le sommeil d’un homme savant vaut mieux que la station d’un ignorant. »

Ce n’est pas une métaphore de paresse, mais une critique du ritualisme sans compréhension.
Le mouvement sans sens n’est pas adoration. Le silence nourri de sagesse vaut mieux que le bruit vide de réflexion.

Depuis 1447 ans, le message de Muhammad ﷺ résonne. Et pourtant, de nombreuses nations — surtout arabes — restent prisonnières des formes héritées.
Elles récitent : « En vérité, Allah ne change pas l’état d’un peuple tant qu’ils ne changent pas ce qui est en eux. » (Coran 13:11)
Mais elles n’ont pas changé.

Leurs mosquées restent figées. Leurs sermons répètent des scripts anciens. Leurs institutions s’accrochent aux quatre écoles comme si elles étaient divines.
Reprenant le cri des ennemis de Noé : « N’abandonnez jamais vos dieux : Wadd, Suwâ’, Ya’uq et Nasr. » (Coran 71:23)

Derrière chaque Pharaon se cache un Hâmân — bâtisseur de tours, gardien de l’orthodoxie.
Le Hâmân d’aujourd’hui n’est pas ministre de briques, mais gardien de jurisprudence figée.
Il étouffe la réflexion, institutionnalise la stagnation, et appelle cela tradition.

Nous rejetons le ritualisme hérité.
Nous revendiquons la révélation comme un code vivant — qui évolue, provoque et réveille.
Nous honorons le sommeil du savant, car son calme est lumière.
Nous défions la station de l’ignorant, car son agitation est vide.

نوم العالم

« نوم العالم خير من قيام الجاهل »

ليست هذه استعارة للكسل، بل نقد للطقوس بلا فهم.
الحركة بلا معنى ليست عبادة. والصمت المملوء بالبصيرة خير من الضجيج الخالي من التأمل.

منذ ١٤٤٧ سنة، ورسالة محمد ﷺ تتردد. ومع ذلك، لا تزال كثير من الأمم — وخاصة العربية — مقيدة بالأشكال الموروثة.
يرددون: « إِنَّ اللَّهَ لَا يُغَيِّرُ مَا بِقَوْمٍ حَتَّى يُغَيِّرُوا مَا بِأَنْفُسِهِمْ » (الرعد: ١١)
لكنهم لم يغيروا شيئًا.

مساجدهم جامدة. خطبهم تكرر نصوصًا موروثة. مؤسساتهم تتمسك بالمذاهب الأربعة وكأنها وحي.
يرددون صرخة أعداء نوح: « وَقَالُوا لَا تَذَرُنَّ آلِهَتَكُمْ وَلَا تَذَرُنَّ وَدًّا وَلَا سُوَاعًا وَلَا يَغُوثَ وَيَعُوقَ وَنَسْرًا » (نوح: ٢٣)

وراء كل فرعون هامان — باني الأبراج، حارس الجمود.
هامان اليوم ليس وزير الطوب، بل حارس الفقه الموروث.
يخنق التساؤل، ويؤسس للركود، ويسميه تقليدًا.

نحن نرفض الطقوس الموروثة.
ونستعيد الوحي كرمز حي — يتطور، ويستفز، ويوقظ.
نكرم نوم العالم، لأن سكونه نور.
ونتحدى قيام الجاهل، لأن حركته خواء.

This is our oath. This is our awakening. This is our legacy.
